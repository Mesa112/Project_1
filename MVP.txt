I will start by creating a Consumer risk analyzer
->Goal
Analyze the text of a consumer agreement and identify potentially unfavorable terms before the user accepts them.
--> Version 1 scope
* Accept text from an agreement.
* Identify potentially concerning clauses.
* Explain why each claude could matter.
* Point to the original wording as evidence.
* Suggest questions the consumer should ask.
* Clearly distinguish confirmed terms form uncertainties
